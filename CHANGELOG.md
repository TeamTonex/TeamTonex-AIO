# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- Updates install quietly in the background and the program starts again on its own, with no window left waiting. This takes effect from the next update after this one.
- Sliders in every tab drag smoothly: grabbing the handle no longer makes it jump, the grab area is bigger, and Shift moves it in finer steps.
- Sign: the preview follows a slider while you drag it, not only after you let go.

## 1.1.0

- Sign: the real Vinewood hill from your own game, with its ground textures, roads, trees and the radio tower, instead of a drawn hill. It is read once, in about half a minute, the first time the Sign tab opens.
- Sign: the frame posts now reach the real ground, in the preview and in the exported sign.
- Sign: a softer sky with haze in the distance, and moonlight at night.
- Clothing: arms show in full under short and slanted sleeves, with no gaps at the hem.
- Bags and clothing: a note when all your imported pieces for a slot are made for the other body.
- Your imported packs and designs are found again when the program starts without its settings.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Bags, Clothing, Vests, Sign and Vehicles studios.
- Emergency lights: draw a line on the car to get light sets made for it, LED strips that follow the body, ready-made sets, and siren bones added to cars without them.
- Mods tab: any gta5-mods.com mod or downloaded file turned into a FiveM resource, with smaller textures.
- Updates from GitHub with one-click install, checked every 30 minutes or from Settings.
